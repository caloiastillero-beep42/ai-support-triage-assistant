AI Support Triage Assistant

An AI-assisted workflow for technical SaaS support teams that helps specialists triage customer issues, investigate problems, assess impact, and make evidence-based escalation decisions.

What this project does

The workflow takes a customer-reported technical issue and helps a support specialist structure the investigation before deciding whether to continue troubleshooting or escalate.

Issue Classification Identifies categories such as API, authentication, billing, transactions, integrations, bugs, and performance.
Customer Impact Assesses whether the issue has low, medium, high, or critical impact.
Evidence Gathering Identifies missing information required to continue the investigation.
Troubleshooting Recommends proportionate, lowest-risk-first investigation steps.
Escalation Determines when the available evidence or business impact justifies involving Engineering.
Engineering Context Structures useful technical information for an engineering escalation.

How it works

The workflow is intentionally designed around a support specialist's existing decision-making process:

Understand → Investigate → Troubleshoot → Resolve → Document

The goal is not to replace human judgment. The AI provides structure, identifies gaps in the available evidence, and helps make support decisions more consistent.

Evaluation scenarios

Transaction Failure Limited evidence → gather more information before escalation.
Webhook Delivery Failure High business impact → investigate the delivery path and establish where the failure occurs.
Webhook HTTP 500 Stronger technical evidence → escalation becomes appropriate.
Critical Production Outage Severe business impact and no workaround → immediate escalation.
Expired API Key Issue resolved with a new key → avoid unnecessary Engineering escalation.
Evidence + Customer Impact → Support Decision

AI Guardrails

  • Never invent customer information, logs, IDs, or error messages.
  • Clearly distinguish evidence from hypotheses.
  • Never present a hypothesis as a confirmed root cause.
  • Never claim an issue was reproduced unless reproduction is provided.
  • Avoid unnecessary customer-side changes.
  • Avoid unsafe or repeated production retries.
  • Never expose credentials, API keys, or sensitive information.
  • Escalate based on evidence and customer impact.
  • Keep the support specialist responsible for the final decision.

Why I built it

I built this project to explore how AI can be applied to real technical support workflows rather than simply generating customer responses.

My background is in SaaS technical support and operations, including technical troubleshooting, incident management, engineering escalations, workflow improvement, knowledge management, and cross-functional collaboration.

This project extends that experience into AI-assisted support tooling and experiments with how AI can reduce repetitive triage work while preserving human judgment.
